# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

- **Target Founder**: Solo Technical Founders, Indie Hackers, and AI Engineers building software products with high architectural complexity.
- **Core Pain Point**: Context-switching overhead between architectural engineering, market research, content distribution, and administrative tasks.
- **Value Proposition**: Autonomous sub-agents operating concurrently under strict human-in-the-loop oversight.
